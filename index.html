Arabic YT Factory

أداة خاصة يستعملها صاحب قناة يوتيوب تعليمية عربية لنشر أفلامه الوثائقية العلمية على قناته هو فقط.

Arabic YT Factory

A private tool used by the owner of an Arabic educational YouTube channel to publish his own science documentaries to his own channel.

It runs on the channel owner's computer and uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to that channel and set their thumbnails. It is not a public service and no other user can sign in.
